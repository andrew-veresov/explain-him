AIexplains your idea

Your AI agent becomes the guide.

Give your personal AI agent a link to the idea. It learns how the idea should be explained, opens the interactive explanation, and answers questions in the agent interface you already use.

No second chat. No unfamiliar interface. Explain Him is the visual explanation; your browser agent is the conversation.

Documents store knowledge.
Explain Him creates understanding.

Turn READMEs, docs, diagrams, and other source materials into a visual explanation your AI agent can navigate and explain.

Explain Him provides the structure, context, and UI capabilities. Your personal agent provides the conversation and reasoning.

WebMCP does not form the answer.

The browser agent reads the page/repository, forms the answer, and uses Explain Him tools only when the visual explanation should focus or adapt.
